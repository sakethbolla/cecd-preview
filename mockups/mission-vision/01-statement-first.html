About/Mission & Vision

A resource for energetics research and manufacturing

Since 1998, the Center for Engineering Concepts Development has served the State of Maryland and the Nation through energetics research, graduate education, and sustained partnerships across government, academia, and industry.

Mission

What the Center does.

The Center's mission is twofold. First, to conduct research on emerging ideas in energetics and engineering, serving as a focal point for work that supports Maryland's contribution to national and economic security.

Second, to bring attention to non-traditional fundamental and applied research problems that could become practical solutions for academia, industry, and government.

Vision

Where the work is headed.

A national resource for the science and manufacturing of energetic materials, and a platform for trying new ideas in engineering education, future technologies, and research.

Just as important is preparing the next generation of scientists and engineers who will carry this work forward through the Center's graduate research and educational programs.

Federal sponsors include

  • Naval Surface Warfare Center Indian Head Division
  • Office of Naval Research
  • Army Research Laboratory
  • and other federal partners
