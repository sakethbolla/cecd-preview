Mission & Vision page
15 layout options

Each option borrows its page structure from an outside research organization's about page (named on the card) and is built on the CECD site's header, footer, fonts, and colors. Mission and vision are one statement in every version. Copy is draft-grade and can be edited in any option.

01 Photo hero, pull quote, approach Full-bleed photo hero with a pull quote, the statement, an "our approach" bullet list of how the work gets done, and a secondary navigation rail. Modeled on The New School, Mission and Vision 02 Poster Two full-width bands in large type: the red band carries the single mission-and-vision statement, the black band says what that means in practice. Almost nothing else on the page. Modeled on Ursa Major style poster bands, redone in plain wording 03 Charter A formal, document-like page on white with a serif pull statement and a Purpose / Scope / Approach list. Reads like an institutional record, not a pitch. Modeled on institutional charter pages (Illinois Tech strategic plan, Kentucky ECE) 04 Tagline and organization One short tagline over a photo, an "Our organization" paragraph carrying the combined statement, three teaser links, and a quiet legacy band. Modeled on MIT Lincoln Laboratory, About 05 Statement and tile grid A two-line statement up top, then a six-tile grid that sends readers to the existing pages. The statement stays short because depth lives elsewhere. Modeled on Johns Hopkins APL, About 06 Three-phrase tagline and purpose A short three-phrase line as the hero, then one "Our purpose" statement, then named values with a one-line gloss each. White page, no photos. Modeled on Beckman Institute, Mission and Core Values 07 Purpose with alternating rows A bold tagline, then alternating text and photo rows (what we do, our people, where we are going). Each row has one bolded claim. Modeled on Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Purpose 08 Photo hero and three cards Photo hero, three equal cards with simple line icons (research, education, partnership), a dark "core competencies" band, and a plain contact line. Modeled on Savannah River National Laboratory, About Us 09 Statement, directions, values, audiences Statement paragraph, three one-line directions, values with a short gloss, and a closing link grid split by audience (researchers, partners, students). Modeled on Ames National Laboratory, Mission and Values 10 Essay with photo themes A one-sentence purpose, an essay-length paragraph, and three named themes, each with a full-bleed photo and one pull line. Long-form and editorial. Modeled on Georgia Tech, Vision 11 Declarative line and narrative One bold declarative line, an "about" narrative paragraph, a bulleted list of what the Center does, and a short "who we serve" list. Modeled on Sandia National Laboratories, About 12 Statement over image, engage row A single statement over an image, one line on where the work focuses, and an "engage" row of links. The shortest page in the set. Modeled on Stanford HAI, home 13 Narrative, director, affiliation, values An "About" narrative, a director photo with a short note, an affiliation banner, named core values, and a link to records. The statement is woven into the prose. Modeled on Penn State Applied Research Laboratory, About 14 What we do, callout, partner story, people A "what we do" paragraph, the statement in a boxed callout, a "trusted partner" origin story, four generic focus areas, and a people grid by role rather than name. Modeled on Carnegie Mellon Software Engineering Institute, About 15 Tagline, statement, origin, places A tagline hero, the statement as a labeled block, the origin story of the 1998 agreement, a plain capabilities list, and the two places the Center works. Modeled on Texas A&M Bush Combat Development Complex
