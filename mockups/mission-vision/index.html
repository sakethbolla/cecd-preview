CECD · University of Maryland

Mission & Vision page
15 layout options

Each option borrows its page structure from the about page of an organization working in energetics, propellants, explosives, or hypersonics in the US or EU (named on the card, with the URL) and is built on the CECD site's header, footer, fonts, and colors. Every text block is a placeholder that says what kind of content belongs there and why it is safe to publish. Mission and vision are one statement in every version. The reference site is shown on each page.

01 Statement, then history Photo hero with a short mission paragraph, a compact "Our history" paragraph right after it, a field-level areas list, and a news row. The history paragraph does the credibility work. Modeled on LLNL Energetic Materials Center, About https://energetics.llnl.gov/about 02 Poster Two full-width bands in large type: the red band carries the single mission-and-vision statement, the black band says what that means in practice, and link tiles replace prose. The format Dylan liked, re-referenced to domain peers. Modeled on FFI Norway (tagline + link tiles) and Missouri S&T Explosives Engineering (three-word tagline) https://www.ffi.no/en/about-ffi 03 Charter A formal, document-like page: a numbered mandate, a "tasked with" bullet list, and a small key-figures block. Reads like an institutional record, not a pitch. The format Dylan liked, re-referenced. Modeled on ONERA, Identity https://www.onera.fr/en/identity 04 Purpose and objectives A white page with one purpose statement and four strategic objectives, one flat sentence each. The calmest register in the set. Modeled on FOI Swedish Defence Research Agency, Vision and Objectives https://www.foi.se/en/foi/about-foi/organization/vision-and-objectives.html 05 Narrative hook, we operate, we promote A short history-flavored opening, the statement, then two twin bullet lists ("We operate" for what exists, "We promote" for what the center works toward), and links to history and visiting. Modeled on DLR Institute of Space Propulsion, About us https://www.dlr.de/en/ra/about-us/the-institute-of-space-propulsion 06 Partnership framing, plain financing, facts The center framed as a university-and-Navy partnership, the statement with two focus areas, applications, a plain "Financing" section that names funders without asking, a small facts block, and contact. Modeled on ISL French-German Research Institute of Saint-Louis, About https://www.isl.eu/en/about 07 Statement, facts bar, three doors A hero mission paragraph, a facts bar right under it, one expertise paragraph, then three links (Research, Partnerships, Students). The closest domain peer to CECD. Modeled on Purdue Energetics Research Center, About https://engineering.purdue.edu/Energetics/about 08 Growth story and partners A founding-and-growth paragraph ("founded in [year] by [name], grown from a small group to a community of faculty and researchers"), a capability paragraph, an unforced list of partner organizations, and a link to the publications archive. Modeled on UAH Propulsion Research Center, About https://www.uah.edu/prc/about 09 Statement and numbered areas The statement as a labeled block, then a numbered list of research areas at field level. UCAH splits Vision and Mission; here they are one block. Modeled on University Consortium for Applied Hypersonics, About us https://hypersonics.tamu.edu/about-us/ 10 Sibling center template The pattern UMD's own Center for Risk and Reliability uses: "Founded in [year], the Center's mission is to [verb phrases]," then a "years of research" line, then people. Same brand, lowest friction. Modeled on UMD Center for Risk and Reliability https://crr.umd.edu/ 11 One sentence page One confident capability sentence, an institutional line, and links. Nothing else. Legitimacy comes from the university and the founding partner, not from the page. Modeled on Sandia Explosive Technology Group https://www.sandia.gov/research/facilities/explosive-technology-group-etg/ 12 Tagline, outputs, focus areas, alignment A tagline, a credibility paragraph built on outputs (papers per year, theses, symposia) rather than money, a focus-areas list, and an "alignment" paragraph on whose needs the work serves. Modeled on DEVCOM Armaments Center https://www.devcom.army.mil/centerslabs/ac/ 13 Director quote, competency tiles, pillars A director photo and quote as the hero, four competency tiles where the field sits as one peer discipline among others, and a short "three pillars of our research" text. Modeled on Fraunhofer Institute for Chemical Technology (ICT) https://www.ict.fraunhofer.de/en.html 14 Education first, partners plain One mission paragraph that leads with education, a short paragraph on how students are taught, a flat sentence naming partner institutions, and navigation to staff and research. Modeled on University of Pardubice, Institute of Energetic Materials https://fcht.upce.cz/en/fcht/iem/about.html 15 Past, present, future The page organized as three parts: how the center started, what it does today, and where it is going. The statement sits in "present." Dates and institutions only in "past," no amounts. Modeled on AFRL Rocket Propulsion Division, past, present and future https://afresearchlab.com/afrls-rocket-lab-past-present-and-future/
