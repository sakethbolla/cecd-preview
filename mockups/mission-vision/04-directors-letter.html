Mission & Vision

Peter W. Chung

Director, Center for Engineering Concepts Development
Professor, Department of Mechanical Engineering

From the Director

The Center for Engineering Concepts Development was established in 1998 so that the University of Maryland could contribute, in a sustained way, to the science and manufacturing of energetic materials.

That purpose has not changed. We conduct research on emerging ideas in energetics and engineering, we prepare graduate students and working professionals for careers in the field, and we bring what we learn to the government, industry, and academic partners who can use it. Much of the work is done with the Naval Surface Warfare Center Indian Head Division and other federal sponsors, and much of it is published in the open literature.

The Center is small by design. It brings faculty together across departments and institutions around problems that matter to Maryland and to the Nation, and it keeps that work moving through research, courses, symposia, and publications.

If you are a student, a researcher, or a partner organization with an interest in energetics, we are glad to hear from you.

Peter W. Chung

Mission

To conduct research on emerging ideas in energetics and engineering, and to bring attention to fundamental and applied problems that could become practical solutions for academia, industry, and government.

Vision

A national resource for energetics science and manufacturing, and a platform for new ideas in engineering education, future technologies, and research.
