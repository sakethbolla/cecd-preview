About the Center

A platform for new ideas in energetics research, education, and technology, put to work through research and symposia since 1998.

Research

Basic, applied, and developmental research in energetics science and manufacturing, carried out with federal sponsors.

Education

Graduate research, courses, and training that prepare students and working professionals for the field.

Partnership

Sustained relationships with government, industry, and academic organizations in Maryland and beyond.

Core competencies

Where the Center's work sits

  • Energetic materials science
  • Manufacturing and processing
  • Testing and characterization
  • Modeling and computation
  • Education and workforce

For further information or to propose collaborations, contact the Center.
