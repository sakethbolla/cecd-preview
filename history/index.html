History & Timeline

The formerly-named Center for Energetic Concepts Development (CECD) was established at the University of Maryland in 1998 through a cooperative agreement with the Naval Surface Warfare Center Indian Head Division (NSWC-IHD). Its vision was to become the preeminent National Center for the science and manufacturing of energetic materials and products for national defense and security, while advancing research, graduate education, technology transfer, and the training of future scientists and engineers in energetics.
