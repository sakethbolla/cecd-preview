Interpretable machine learning models shock compression from scarce data.

Balakrishnan, VanGessel, Barnes, and Chung train machine learning on sparse experimental data to recover the shock compression behavior of solids, where conventional fits run short of data.

Liu and Chung uncover thermal transport in molecular crystals that departs from conventional expectations, with implications for how heat moves through energetic solids.

Energetic Materials

J. Phys. Chem. A · 2021

Three-phonon pathways carry vibrational energy through crystalline RDX.

Kumar, VanGessel, Munday, and Chung map the 3-phonon scattering pathways that move vibrational energy through crystalline RDX, a step toward understanding how energetic crystals respond before initiation.
